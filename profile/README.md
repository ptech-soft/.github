### 🌐 About Us
**PTech Soft** is a technology startup founded in **October 2025**, focused on building scalable cloud systems, intelligent AI applications, and robust B2B platforms. We specialize in transforming complex business workflows into seamless digital experiences.

---

### 💡 Core Focus & Products
- 🏨 **Roomrise / PMS**: Comprehensive Property Management System automating reservations, channel management, and guest experiences.
- 🤖 **AI Automation & Agents**: AI workflows, edge inference, and intelligent business automation solutions.
- ⚡ **Fullstack & Cloud Engineering**: High-performance architectures built with Next.js, Laravel, Node.js, and serverless edge computing.

---

### 🛠️ Tech Stack & Ecosystem
- **Frontend & Fullstack**: Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript
- **Backend & APIs**: Laravel (PHP), Python, Node.js
- **Cloud & Edge Infrastructure**: Cloudflare (Workers, R2, D1, WAF), Linux VPS, Docker
- **Databases & Cache**: PostgreSQL, MySQL, Redis, Vector Databases
